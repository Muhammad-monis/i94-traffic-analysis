# 🚦 I-94 Traffic Volume Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes traffic volume data from the I-94 Interstate Highway to identify key indicators of heavy congestion.

The goal is to determine whether traffic patterns are influenced by:
- Time-based factors (month, day of week, hour)
- Weather conditions

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Identify peak traffic periods
- Compare weekday vs weekend traffic
- Analyze seasonal traffic patterns
- Investigate weather conditions associated with heavy traffic

---

## 📊 Dataset
The dataset was made available by John Hogue and can be found on the UCI Machine Learning Repository.

It includes:
- Date and time
- Traffic volume
- Temperature
- Rain and snow measurements
- Weather type

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Jupyter Notebook
-  matplotlib

---

## 🔍 Key Findings

### ⏰ Time-Based Indicators
- Traffic volume is higher during warm months (March–October)
- Traffic is heavier on business days compared to weekends
- Peak congestion occurs around 7 AM and 4 PM (rush hours)

### 🌦 Weather-Based Indicators
The following weather conditions are associated with heavier traffic:
- Shower snow
- Light rain and snow
- Proximity thunderstorm with drizzle

---

## ✅ Conclusion
Heavy traffic on I-94 is strongly influenced by both time and weather conditions. 
Rush hours on business days and specific adverse weather events significantly increase traffic volume.
